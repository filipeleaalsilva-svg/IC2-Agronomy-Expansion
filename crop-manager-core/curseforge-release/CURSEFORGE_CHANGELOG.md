# Crop Manager Core 0.5.0-alpha9

## English

### Highlights
- **Initial CurseForge Core Release** packaging the complete IC2 Experimental (`2.8.222-ex112`) crop automation suite for Minecraft 1.12.2:
  - **Advanced Crop Manager** (harvesting, fertilizing, hydration, Weed-Ex, 3D configurable area, environmental census).
  - **Agricultural Monitor** (wireless telemetry, remote controls, automation hysteresis rules, redstone alarm/status output, wireless logistics).
  - **Electric Weed Harvester** (dedicated EU-powered IC2 weed removal with 4 output slots).
  - **Electric Composter** (organic items or IC2 weeds + water → IC2 Fertilizer).
  - **Weed-Ex Mixer** (3-tank automated synthesis of IC2 Weed-Ex from Spider Eyes, Redstone, and Water).
  - **Crop Database Chest & Portable Crop Database** (500-slot IC2 seed storage with search, genetics display, and Favorites).
- **Universal Wireless EU Upgrade:** Implements `IUpgradeItem` for both Crop Manager and compatible IC2 machines (`Shift + Right-Click` on `BatBox`, `CESU`, `MFE`, or `MFSU` to link).
- **IC2 Upgrade & Battery Support:** 4 IC2 upgrade slots and auto-discharging battery slots on support machines.
- **Optional Dark GUI Resource Pack:** `CropManagerCore-GUI-Dark-0.5.0-alpha9.zip` included as an additional file.
- **Localization:** Full `en_us` and `pt_br` language parity.
